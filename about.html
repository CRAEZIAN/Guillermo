About Me

The artist behind the screen

I’m Crazian, a self-taught multimedia artist from Cavite who thinks with a pencil in hand first and a keyboard second. Fresh grad, cafe hunter, and a creative who can’t stick to one medium.

Main medium

Traditional art

This is where everything starts. Paper, pencil, ink and paint: the slow, messy, hands-on way of making. Every other skill I have grew out of it, and my art account @crazian_art is where those pieces live.
